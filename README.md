# My Favorite Movie: The Fast and the Furious (2001)

## Overview
*The Fast and the Furious* is an action film directed by Rob Cohen. It marks the beginning of the iconic franchise centered on street racing, undercover operations, and family.

---

## Key Characters
* **Dominic Toretto** (Vin Diesel) – The leader of a skilled street racing crew and quarter-mile racer.
* **Brian O'Conner** (Paul Walker) – An LAPD officer who goes undercover in the street racing scene.
* **Mia Toretto** (Jordana Brewster) – Dom's sister who runs the family grocery store and diner.
* **Letty Ortiz** (Michelle Rodriguez) – Dom's girlfriend and a highly skilled racer and mechanic.

---

## Notable Cars Featured
1. **1970 Dodge Charger R/T** – Dom's legendary muscle car built with his father.
2. **1995 Toyota Supra MK IV** – The iconic 2JZ-powered restoration project built in Dom's garage.
3. **1995 Mitsubishi Eclipse GS** – Brian's initial street racing car.
4. **1995 Mazda RX-7 FD3S** – Dom's primary street racer early in the film.

---

## Favorite Quote
> *"I live my life a quarter-mile at a time. Nothing else matters... For those ten seconds or less, I'm free."* — Dominic Toretto
